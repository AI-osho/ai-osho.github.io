Twenty seconds of it

Four fighters, one stage, no health bars. Damage builds a percentage — the higher yours climbs, the further the next hit sends you.

What's in it

Eight fighters

Two humans, two robots, two beasts, two monsters. Four unique specials each — thirty-two in all, and none of them play alike.

Quest mode

Four chapters, sixteen fights, four bosses. Every node tells you what it pays before you commit, and bosses drop rare gear.

Gear & skills

Ninety-six pieces of equipment that show up on the character, not just in the stats. Level a special and it changes, not just hits harder.

Local versus

Two to four fighters on one device, or against the CPU at four difficulty levels. Stocks from one to five, or endless.

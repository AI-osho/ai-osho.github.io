POP CLASH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

POP CLASH does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The game has no network functionality of any kind.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDK that transmits data. The app requests no runtime permissions: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or identifiers.

What the app stores on your device

One save file, held in the app's own private storage on your device. It contains your coins, the equipment you own and have equipped, your skill levels, your quest progress, your chosen language and a count of matches played and won. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. The Android INTERNET permission is present because it is declared by the application framework the game is built on; the game itself never uses it, and the app runs identically in airplane mode.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and contains no advertising, no purchases and no communication features. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
